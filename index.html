Good questions

Everything you need
to know before pickup.

Can you take my trash cans to the curb every week?+

Yes. Weekly trash can curb service is our most popular option. We move accessible bins out before collection and can return them after the truck passes.

Do you offer trash can service for seniors in Surprise?+

Yes. Our Surprise, AZ curb-and-return service can help seniors, residents with limited mobility, caregivers, and busy households manage collection day.

Can you move my trash bins while I’m out of town?+

Yes. Vacation trash bin service is available for Surprise homes so your normal collection routine can continue while you are away.

What happens if my collection day changes?+

Update your request with the new day. Once service is confirmed, schedule-change instructions will be included with your plan.

Do I need to be home?+

No. As long as the bins are accessible, service can be completed without anyone home.

Can you handle more than four bins?+

Absolutely. Choose “4+ bins” in the estimator and add the exact count in your booking notes for a custom quote.

How does online payment work?+

After you submit your service details, Stripe securely collects payment for the plan you selected. Trash Grab Express does not receive or store your full card number.

Is this a long-term contract?+

No. Recurring plans renew monthly and are designed to stay flexible. Final cancellation and pause details are included when service is confirmed.
